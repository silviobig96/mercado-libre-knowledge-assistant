# NovaRetail AI Assistant Demo Script

1. Open Admin.
2. Upload the NovaRetail PDF with policies, procedures, warranties, logistics, or FAQs.
3. Confirm the document appears in Uploaded Documents with chunk count.
4. Open Chat.
5. Click a suggested question or type a natural-language question.
6. Show the grounded answer and the Sources section.
7. Open Business Flow to explain how the assistant fits NovaRetail operations.
8. Open Architecture to explain Activity 2 solution design.
9. Open Evaluation to explain Activity 5 metrics, impact, risks, and mitigations.

## Presentation Notes

- This is not an ecommerce storefront.
- NovaRetail is the business context for an internal knowledge assistant.
- The prototype demonstrates document ingestion, embeddings, semantic search, grounded generation, and source attribution.
