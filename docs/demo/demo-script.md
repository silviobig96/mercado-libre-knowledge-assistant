# NovaRetail AI Assistant Demo Script

1. Open the NovaRetail Knowledge Center landing page.
2. Go to Admin and sign in with the demo admin password.
3. Upload the NovaRetail PDF with policies, procedures, warranties, logistics, or FAQs.
4. Select the document category.
5. Confirm the document appears in Uploaded Documents with category and chunk count.
6. Open Chat.
7. Ask a suggested question in Spanish.
8. Ask a suggested question in English.
9. Ask an out-of-scope question to demonstrate the fallback.
10. Open the Sources accordion and show source attribution.
11. Mark a response as Helpful or Not helpful.
12. Open Evaluation to show document, query, and feedback metrics.
13. Open Business Flow to explain how the assistant fits NovaRetail operations.
14. Open Architecture to explain Activity 2 solution design.

## Presentation Notes

- This is not an ecommerce storefront.
- NovaRetail is the business context for an internal knowledge assistant.
- The prototype demonstrates document ingestion, embeddings, semantic search, grounded generation, and source attribution.
